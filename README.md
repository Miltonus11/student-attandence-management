# SITS Attendance Management System

A school-based platform designed to record, manage, and monitor student attendance efficiently for admins, teachers, and students.

## Description

SITS Attendance Management System is a system designed to help the school record and monitor student attendance in a more organized way. The system has three users: the Admin (managed by the Registrar), the Teacher, and the Student. 

The Admin is responsible for setting up and maintaining all the main data in the system, such as student information, teacher accounts, subjects, and sections, and also handles the assignment of subjects to teachers and the placement of students in their correct sections. 

Once everything is set, teachers can log in to view their assigned subjects and sections, and they can record attendance by marking each student as present, absent, late, or excused. Their submissions are saved in the system and become part of the student's attendance record. 

Students have a simpler interface where they can log in and view their attendance status, including their absences and other remarks, but they cannot edit or change any data. 

Overall, the system provides a straightforward and centralized way for the registrar to manage records, for teachers to take attendance efficiently, and for students to stay updated on their attendance.

## User Workflows

### Admin Workflow

The Admin sets up the system by adding students and teachers. Subjects are created with a specific teacher assigned to handle them, and sections are created with a subject attached and filled with the appropriate students. Once everything is set, teachers can log in to view the subjects assigned to them and record attendance by marking each student as present or absent. Students have a view-only panel where they can see their attendance status and track their absences. The system centralizes all attendance records, allowing all users to access accurate and updated information easily.

**Key Features:**
- View admin dashboard with current counts of teachers, students, subjects, and sections
- See daily statistics of student absences and attendance
- Add and manage students and teachers in the system
- Create subjects and assign each subject to a specific teacher
- Create sections, assign subjects to each section, and enroll students

### Teacher Workflow

The teacher workflow begins with the instructor logging in from the landing page and entering the dashboard, where all their assigned subjects are displayed. From there, the instructor selects a subject to view its schedules and associated sections, then chooses a specific section to see detailed class information, including the list of enrolled students and their attendance status. By clicking on a student, the instructor can access individual attendance records and performance details. The instructor can then update or mark attendance for the current session as needed. Additional options such as editing their profile or adjusting system preferences are available in the settings menu, and once finished, the instructor securely logs out of the system.

**Process Flow:**
1. **Landing Page** - The instructor starts at the landing page, where they log in to access the system
2. **Dashboard** - After logging in, the instructor is taken to the dashboard, which displays the list of subjects they handle
3. **View Subjects** - The instructor selects a subject from the list to view its details, including schedules and assigned sections
4. **Select Section** - After choosing a subject, the instructor views the available sections and selects one to see the enrolled students
5. **View Section Details** - The system displays the full section information, including student list, attendance status, and class details
6. **Click Student** - When a student is clicked, the instructor can view detailed information such as attendance records and performance indicators
7. **Update Attendance** - The instructor marks or updates student attendance for the selected class session

### Student Workflow

The student workflow in the Attendance Management System begins at the landing page, where the student logs in using their credentials and is directed to the main dashboard. From the dashboard, the student can view their enrolled subjects, check their attendance summary, and navigate through detailed attendance records such as daily status, total absences, and class schedules. By selecting a subject, the student can view a full attendance calendar that visually displays their presence, absences, and reminders for the semester. The system also allows students to manage their profile and account settings before logging out to securely end their session.

**Process Flow:**
1. **Landing Page** - The student opens the system and is greeted with the landing page before logging in
2. **Login** - The student enters their credentials to access their personalized dashboard
3. **Dashboard** - After logging in, the student views their attendance summary, subjects, and profile information
4. **View Subjects** - The student selects a subject from the menu to see more detailed attendance information
5. **Attendance Calendar** - The student views a full calendar showing their daily attendance status for the selected subject
6. **Attendance Details** - The student clicks on specific dates or records to see complete attendance details and instructor notes

### Settings

### Logout
The Logout option securely signs the user out of the system and returns them to the login screen. This helps protect user information and prevents unauthorized access by ensuring that accounts are not left open after use.
